Our Story

Built on Expertise, Driven by Results

TwinStack Solutions was founded in 2022 in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a single focus: delivering high-quality, results-driven Salesforce solutions that help organisations reach their full CRM potential.

From our first project to a portfolio spanning the US, Australia, Canada and Europe, we have kept the same promise: deep technical expertise paired with a client-first mindset. Every solution is tailored, every delivery is on time, and every relationship is built to last. Alongside client work, we publish free native apps on the Salesforce AppExchange.

Our Parent Company

A TwinForce Company

TwinStack Solutions is a sister concern of TwinForce Solutions Limited, a full-service software company based in Dhaka. TwinForce builds software across the stack; TwinStack is the group's dedicated Salesforce company, focused entirely on Salesforce consulting, implementation and AppExchange products. Clients get a specialist Salesforce team, backed by the people, processes and wider engineering capability of the TwinForce group.
