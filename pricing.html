Pricing questions

Is the first call free?

Yes. The first 30-minute call is free. We look at what you need and tell you how we would approach it before any paid work starts.

How does a monthly hours plan work?

We agree a baseline number of hours a month with you, usually over a 12-month term. Each month we plan the work together, and you are billed for the hours we deliver at US$30 (A$47) an hour.

What if we need more hours in a month?

Tell us ahead of a busy period and we scale up. Additional hours are billed at the same rate, only when you have approved them.

How do we know where the hours went?

We track hours against each piece of work and report them to you every month, so every invoice can be checked against the work delivered.

Are Salesforce licences included?

No. Salesforce licences and any third-party apps are paid to their vendors, separately from our hours.

Why is there a separate Australian dollar rate?

We bill Australian clients in Australian dollars at A$47 an hour, so there are no currency conversions on your invoice.

How do I get an estimate for a project?

Call us and tell us what you need. We estimate projects in hours at our standard rate, line by line, so you can check every part before work starts.
