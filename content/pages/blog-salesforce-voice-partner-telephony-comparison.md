<h2 id="choosing-the-right-fit">Choosing the right fit</h2>
<p>Ask three questions before you shortlist:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li>Do you want a single vendor, or do you already run a contact centre or UCaaS platform you want to keep?</li>
<li>Which AI and workforce features are essential to your operation?</li>
<li>Does any documented limitation break an agent workflow you rely on?</li>
</ol>
<p>As a starting point: Amazon Connect for simplicity, NICE or Five9 for enterprise scale, Zoom or RingCX if you are already on their UCaaS, Talkdesk for fast deployment, and Twilio for developer flexibility. Always test the limitations against your own workflows.</p>
<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 class="imp-q">What is partner telephony in Salesforce Voice?</h3>
<p>It means using your own contact centre provider, such as Zoom, NICE, Talkdesk, Five9, RingCentral or Twilio, with Salesforce Voice, instead of Amazon Connect supplied by Salesforce.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Which Salesforce Voice option has one bill?</h3>
<p>The Salesforce-managed Amazon Connect model, where Salesforce provides and bills the telephony. Partner options usually mean two vendors and two bills.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Do all partners support Omni-Channel?</h3>
<p>All the options here embed calls through Omni-Channel, but they differ in which Omni-Channel features, such as Flows and skills routing, they support.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Which option is best?</h3>
<p>It depends on your existing platforms, the AI and workforce features you need, and whether any documented limitation breaks your workflows.</p>
