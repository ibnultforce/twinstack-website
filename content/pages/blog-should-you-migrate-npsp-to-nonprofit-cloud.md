                <p class="imp-text-xl imp-text-slate-600 imp-font-medium imp-mb-8"><strong>Short answer:</strong> not automatically. NPSP is still supported and Salesforce has not announced an end-of-life date, so moving is a decision, not a deadline. Move when Nonprofit Cloud solves a problem you actually have, usually programme or case management, a messy gift process, grantmaking, or a need for Salesforce's new AI features. Stay when your NPSP org is stable, your integrations depend on it, and you have no budget for a rebuild this year. Either way, start cleaning your data now, because that work pays off whichever you choose.</p>
                <h2 id="why-this-is-a-real-decision-not-a-forced-one">Why this is a real decision, not a forced one</h2>
<p>Because there is no in-place upgrade, moving from NPSP to Nonprofit Cloud means building a new org and migrating into it. That costs real money and staff time. Some partners frame the move as inevitable. It may be eventually, but "eventually" is not a business case. The case has to come from what the move does for your organisation in the next two to three years.</p>

<h2 id="eight-signals-it-is-time-to-move">Eight signals it is time to move</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>You run programmes or case work outside Salesforce.</strong> Spreadsheets for attendance, a separate case tool, or the Program Management Module stretched past what it was built for. Program and case management are native in Nonprofit Cloud.</li>
<li><strong>Your gift processing is fragile.</strong> Batches are keyed by hand, reconciled in Excel, and occasionally double-counted. Nonprofit Cloud's Gift Entry stages gifts and dry-runs a batch before anything posts.</li>
<li><strong>You give grants.</strong> Nonprofit Cloud for Grantmaking handles applications, reviews, awards and disbursements. NPSP has no equivalent.</li>
<li><strong>You want Salesforce's nonprofit AI agents.</strong> Prospect Research, Participant Management and the other Agentforce Nonprofit agents are built for the new data model.</li>
<li><strong>Your NPSP org is heavily customised and hard to maintain.</strong> If a rebuild is coming anyway, rebuilding on the current platform is better than rebuilding on the old one.</li>
<li><strong>Your key integrations already support Nonprofit Cloud.</strong> Check your payment processor, email tool and forms. If they support Nonprofit Cloud objects, a major blocker is gone.</li>
<li><strong>You are about to grow your Salesforce footprint.</strong> Adding portals, programmes or many more users is cheaper to do once, on the platform you will keep.</li>
<li><strong>A funder will pay for capacity building.</strong> Implementation is a one-off cost that suits capacity-building grants.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="five-signals-it-is-fine-to-wait">Five signals it is fine to wait</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Your org works and your team trusts it.</strong> A stable system with clean data is worth a lot.</li>
<li><strong>Critical tools only support NPSP.</strong> Moving before your payment or event tool supports Nonprofit Cloud means rebuilding that integration yourself.</li>
<li><strong>You have no budget or staff time this year.</strong> A half-funded migration is worse than no migration.</li>
<li><strong>Your data needs serious cleaning first.</strong> Migrating dirty data into a new model makes it harder to fix. Clean first, migrate second.</li>
<li><strong>Your fundraising calendar is full.</strong> Do not cut over during year-end giving or a major appeal.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="a-simple-scoring-exercise">A simple scoring exercise</h2>
<p>Give each of the eight move signals a score from 0 (not true) to 2 (very true), then do the same for the five wait signals.</p>
<div class="imp-article-table-wrap"><table><thead><tr><th>Move score minus wait score</th><th>What it suggests</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>6 or more</td><td>Start planning a migration now</td></tr><tr><td>1 to 5</td><td>Plan for the next 12 to 24 months, and clean data now</td></tr><tr><td>0 or below</td><td>Stay on NPSP and review again next year</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>This is a conversation starter for your board or leadership team, not a formula. One strong signal, such as needing grantmaking, can outweigh everything else.</p>
<h2 id="what-to-do-now-if-you-decide-to-wait">What to do now if you decide to wait</h2>
<p>Waiting well is different from doing nothing. These steps make a future move cheaper and safer:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Clean your donor data.</strong> Deduplicate, standardise addresses and fix household structures. See <a href="/blog/nonprofit-donor-data-cleanup-salesforce.html">donor data clean-up before a Salesforce migration</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Document your automations.</strong> List every flow, process, trigger and integration and what it is for. This list becomes your rebuild scope.</li>
<li><strong>Stop adding custom objects</strong> where a Nonprofit Cloud standard object will exist later.</li>
<li><strong>Retire what nobody uses:</strong> unused fields, reports and permission sets. Our <a href="/blog/how-to-run-a-salesforce-access-review.html">access review guide</a> helps with the permissions side.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="what-to-do-now-if-you-decide-to-move">What to do now if you decide to move</h2>
<p>Start with a scope, not a quote. Map your current objects to their Nonprofit Cloud equivalents using our <a href="/blog/npsp-to-nonprofit-cloud-data-mapping.html">data mapping guide</a>, then price the hours with our <a href="/blog/npsp-to-nonprofit-cloud-migration-cost-timeline.html">timeline and cost guide</a>. If you need a partner, our <a href="/blog/choosing-a-nonprofit-salesforce-consultant.html">ten questions for choosing a nonprofit Salesforce consultant</a> will help you compare proposals.</p>
                <h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 class="imp-q">Do we have to migrate from NPSP to Nonprofit Cloud?</h3>
<p>No. Salesforce has not announced an end-of-life date for NPSP, and existing orgs remain supported. Migration is optional, but new features are going to Nonprofit Cloud, now sold as Agentforce Nonprofit.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">When is the right time to move from NPSP to Nonprofit Cloud?</h3>
<p>When Nonprofit Cloud solves a problem you have now, such as programme management, gift processing or grantmaking, and you have the budget and staff time to rebuild properly. Avoid cutting over during peak fundraising periods.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">What are the risks of moving to Nonprofit Cloud too early?</h3>
<p>The main risks are integrations that only support NPSP, migrating unclean data into a new model, and underfunding the project so that reports and automations are only half rebuilt.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">What should we do if we stay on NPSP for now?</h3>
<p>Clean your data, document every automation and integration, avoid new custom objects that Nonprofit Cloud will replace, and retire unused fields and reports. All of this lowers the cost of a future move.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Can a small nonprofit stay on NPSP?</h3>
<p>Yes. Many small nonprofits run well on NPSP and have no immediate reason to move. Review the decision each year, especially before taking on programmes, portals or grants.</p>
