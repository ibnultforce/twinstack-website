<h1>Salesforce support from a team that knows your org</h1><p class="imp-lead">A monthly hours plan with a named team for admin requests, fixes, small enhancements and release testing, so your Salesforce keeps improving after go-live.</p>
<div class="imp-ctas"><a class="imp-btn imp-bp" href="https://calendar.app.google/HP3LiuNFisCt6xLo6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Book a free call</a><a class="imp-btn imp-bg" href="#contact">Send us a message</a></div></div>
<aside class="imp-sv-facts" aria-label="Key facts"><dl><div><dt>Billing</dt><dd>Monthly, on hours delivered</dd></div><div><dt>Rate</dt><dd>US$30 (A$47) an hour</dd></div><div><dt>Contact</dt><dd>One point of contact through our Architect</dd></div></dl></aside></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="what"><div class="imp-w"><h2>What we do</h2><div class="imp-sv-list"><div><h3>Admin requests</h3><p>New users, permission changes, fields, page layouts, reports and dashboards.</p></div><div><h3>Fixes and troubleshooting</h3><p>Broken Flows, failing integrations and errors your users report, investigated and fixed.</p></div><div><h3>Enhancements</h3><p>Small improvements every month instead of waiting for a large project.</p></div><div><h3>Release readiness</h3><p>Salesforce updates every org three times a year. We review the changes and test what matters to you.</p></div><div><h3>Access reviews</h3><p>Regular checks of who can see and edit what, using our own Who Sees What app.</p></div><div><h3>Health checks and documentation</h3><p>Periodic reviews of your org and up-to-date notes on how it is set up.</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec imp-tint" id="process"><div class="imp-w"><h2>How it works</h2><ol class="imp-sv-steps"><li><h3>Onboard</h3><p>We review your org, document it and agree priorities.</p></li><li><h3>Plan</h3><p>Requests go into a shared list that you prioritise.</p></li><li><h3>Deliver</h3><p>We work through the list each month within your hours.</p></li><li><h3>Report</h3><p>A monthly summary of what was done and what is next.</p></li></ol></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="why"><div class="imp-w"><h2>Why TwinStack</h2><div class="imp-sv-why"><div><h3>One team, not a ticket queue</h3><p>The same people work on your org each month, so you do not explain things twice.</p></div><div><h3>Certified architects behind the team</h3><p>Complex questions go to our architects, not just the person on shift.</p></div><div><h3>Simple billing</h3><p>A baseline of hours you agree, at US$30 (A$47) an hour, with every hour tracked and reported. For example, 30 hours a month is US$900 (A$1,410).</p></div></div><div style="margin-top:3.5rem"><h2 style="font-size:1.5rem">Related</h2><div class="imp-sv-rel"><a href="/pricing.html">Pricing<span>Monthly hours plans and hourly work</span></a><a href="/products/who-sees-what.html">Who Sees What<span>The access explorer we use in reviews</span></a><a href="/services/hire-salesforce-developer.html">Hire a Salesforce developer<span>Add a developer to your own team</span></a><a href="/services/salesforce-implementation.html">Salesforce implementation<span>Bigger changes and new clouds</span></a></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec imp-tint" id="faq"><div class="imp-w"><h2>Common questions</h2><div class="imp-sv-faq"><details><summary>What does Salesforce managed services include?</summary><p>Admin requests, fixes, small enhancements, release testing, access reviews and documentation, delivered by a named team on a monthly hours plan.</p></details><details><summary>How is Salesforce support billed?</summary><p>We agree a baseline of hours a month at US$30 (A$47) an hour, usually over 12 months, and bill monthly on the hours delivered. For example, 30 hours is US$900 (A$1,410). In busier periods we scale up when you approve it, at the same rate.</p></details><details><summary>Who will work on our org?</summary><p>A small named team: an admin or developer for day-to-day work, with an architect available for design questions.</p></details><details><summary>Can you support an org another partner built?</summary><p>Yes. We start with a review and documentation of your org, then take over support from there.</p></details><details><summary>Do you handle Salesforce release updates?</summary><p>Yes. We review each release, test the features and changes that affect you, and help with release updates that need action.</p></details><details><summary>Do you work in our time zone?</summary><p>We work with clients in North America, Europe and Australia and arrange meetings and urgent support around your hours.</p></details></div></div></section>
