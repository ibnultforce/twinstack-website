                <p class="imp-text-xl imp-text-slate-600 imp-font-medium imp-mb-8"><strong>Short answer:</strong> the Salesforce Voice licence is only one layer of the bill. Add telephony, usage, any partner seats, AI extras and implementation, and compare options on the same monthly and first-year total cost of ownership (TCO).</p>
<p>There are three common ways to buy it. Salesforce can supply Amazon Connect for you (a Salesforce-managed bundle). You can bring your own Amazon Connect account (BYOA). Or you can bring your own telephony from a partner such as Zoom, NICE, Talkdesk, Five9, RingCentral or Twilio (BYOT).</p>
<p>Prices are list prices as published in autumn 2026 and change often. Confirm current pricing with Salesforce, AWS and each vendor before you quote or sign.</p>

<h2 id="amazon-connect-billing-the-important-distinction">3. Amazon Connect billing: the important distinction</h2>
<p>A Salesforce-managed bundle includes platform minutes, but telephone network charges and overage are separate. Sizing a bundle only on agent talk time can understate what you use.</p>
<p>With your own Amazon Connect account (BYOA), AWS rates apply. On the Connect Basic plan that is about $0.018 a minute for voice, $0.015 a minute for conversational analytics, plus telephony and phone number charges. <h2 id="building-a-total-cost-model">5. Building a total cost model</h2>
<p>Compare every option on the same two numbers:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Monthly TCO:</strong> base CRM, Salesforce Voice, partner platform, telephony, AI, infrastructure and support.</li>
<li><strong>First-year TCO:</strong> 12 x monthly TCO, plus implementation, number porting, migration and training.</li>
</ul>
<p>Avoid counting anything twice that a signed bundle already includes, and get written quotes before you decide.</p>
<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 class="imp-q">How much does Salesforce Voice cost per user?</h3>
<p>List prices run from $50 per user per month (Partner Contact Center, bring your own telephony) to $200 for the Amazon Connect tier with 5,000 minutes, on top of the Service Cloud licence.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Is Amazon Connect usage included?</h3>
<p>The Salesforce-managed tiers include a set number of platform minutes per user. Telephony charges and overage are billed separately.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Is BYOA cheaper than a managed bundle?</h3>
<p>It can be at some volumes, but it depends on your minutes, analytics use and the effort of running your own AWS account. Compare full monthly and first-year TCO.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Do partner prices include the Salesforce licence?</h3>
<p>No. With partner telephony you pay the partner's seat price and the $50 Salesforce Voice licence, plus any connector fees.</p>
