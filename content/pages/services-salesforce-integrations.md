<h1>Salesforce integrations that keep every system in step</h1><p class="imp-lead">We connect Salesforce to the tools you already run, from payments and accounting to marketing, forms, telephony and single sign-on, with error handling so nothing fails silently.</p>
<div class="imp-ctas"><a class="imp-btn imp-bp" href="https://calendar.app.google/HP3LiuNFisCt6xLo6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Book a free call</a><a class="imp-btn imp-bg" href="#contact">Send us a message</a></div></div>
<aside class="imp-sv-facts" aria-label="Key facts"><dl><div><dt>Approaches</dt><dd>MuleSoft, REST APIs, Zapier and native apps</dd></div><div><dt>Examples</dt><dd>Stripe, QuickBooks, Mailchimp, Amazon Connect</dd></div><div><dt>Rate</dt><dd>US$30 (A$47) an hour</dd></div></dl></aside></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="what"><div class="imp-w"><h2>What we do</h2><div class="imp-sv-list"><div><h3>Payments and donations</h3><p>Stripe and Shopify payments recorded against the right contact, gift or order in Salesforce.</p></div><div><h3>Finance</h3><p>Accounting systems such as QuickBooks kept in step with what is in Salesforce.</p></div><div><h3>Marketing</h3><p>Mailchimp and Marketing Cloud audiences, consent and engagement synced with your records.</p></div><div><h3>Forms and surveys</h3><p>Google Forms, FormAssembly, SurveyMonkey and website forms feeding Salesforce directly.</p></div><div><h3>Telephony and service</h3><p>Service Cloud Voice with Amazon Connect, so agents see the caller's record as the call starts.</p></div><div><h3>Identity and other systems</h3><p>Single sign-on with SAML, plus sector systems such as Yardi and Clever through their APIs.</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec imp-tint" id="process"><div class="imp-w"><h2>How it works</h2><ol class="imp-sv-steps"><li><h3>Map</h3><p>List the systems, the data that moves and which system owns each field.</p></li><li><h3>Choose the approach</h3><p>Native app, middleware or custom API, based on volume, cost and how real-time it needs to be.</p></li><li><h3>Build and test</h3><p>Integration with logging, retries and alerts, tested with real edge cases.</p></li><li><h3>Monitor</h3><p>Handover with documentation, and support when a connected system changes.</p></li></ol></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="why"><div class="imp-w"><h2>Why TwinStack</h2><div class="imp-sv-why"><div><h3>MuleSoft and API experience</h3><p>Our developers build MuleSoft, Apex REST and webhook integrations as everyday work.</p></div><div><h3>We build connectors</h3><p>Google Form Auto Sync, our free app, connects Google Forms to Salesforce without middleware.</p></div><div><h3>Clear pricing</h3><p>We work at US$30 (A$47) an hour and recommend the cheapest approach that is reliable enough.</p></div></div><div style="margin-top:3.5rem"><h2 style="font-size:1.5rem">Related</h2><div class="imp-sv-rel"><a href="/products/google-form-auto-sync.html">Google Form Auto Sync<span>Free native Google Forms connector</span></a><a href="/blog/google-forms-salesforce-methods-compared.html">Every way to connect Google Forms<span>Methods compared side by side</span></a><a href="/blog/google-forms-salesforce-integration-security.html">Is your integration secure?<span>Six questions to ask any vendor</span></a><a href="/services/agentforce-ai.html">Agentforce and AI<span>Agents that act on integrated data</span></a></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec imp-tint" id="faq"><div class="imp-w"><h2>Common questions</h2><div class="imp-sv-faq"><details><summary>Should we use MuleSoft or Zapier?</summary><p>Zapier suits low volumes and simple steps. MuleSoft suits higher volumes, complex transformations and several connected systems. For many single connections, a direct API integration or a native app costs less than either.</p></details><details><summary>Can you integrate Salesforce with Stripe or QuickBooks?</summary><p>Yes. We record payments against the right records in Salesforce and keep finance systems in step, with clear rules about which system owns each field.</p></details><details><summary>Should the integration be real time?</summary><p>Only where the business needs it. Real-time integrations cost more to build and run, so we use scheduled syncs where a delay is acceptable.</p></details><details><summary>What happens when an integration fails?</summary><p>We build logging, retries and alerts so failures are visible and can be replayed, instead of data quietly going missing.</p></details><details><summary>Can you build a custom API for Salesforce?</summary><p>Yes. We build Apex REST services and use the standard Salesforce APIs, secured with OAuth and a dedicated integration user.</p></details><details><summary>How do you keep integrations secure?</summary><p>We use OAuth instead of stored passwords, give each integration user only the access it needs, and document every connection.</p></details></div></div></section>
