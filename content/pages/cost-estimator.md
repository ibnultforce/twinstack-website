        <section class="imp-ce-sec imp-alt" id="whats-included">
            <div class="imp-ce-wrap">
                <div class="imp-ce-center">
                    <span class="imp-ce-kicker">The Hidden Half</span>
                    <h2 class="imp-ce-h2">What the sticker price does not cover</h2>
                    <p class="imp-ce-sub">Not one of the 19 Salesforce contracts we analysed contains a single line of professional services. Salesforce paper is licences. Everything below is a separate cost, and it is usually the larger half of year one.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="imp-ce-cols">
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">01</span><h3>Implementation</h3><p>Configuration, data model, automation, permissions, testing. Industry benchmark is <strong>1.5&times; to 3&times; your annual licence spend</strong>. A quickstart runs $10K&ndash;$23K; an enterprise multi-cloud build runs $150K&ndash;$300K+.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">02</span><h3>Data migration</h3><p>Extraction, cleansing, deduplication, mapping and validation. $5K&ndash;$12K for a clean single-source move, $30K&ndash;$60K when consolidating several legacy systems.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">03</span><h3>Integrations</h3><p>$10K&ndash;$25K per standard REST or iPaaS connection. $50K&ndash;$100K+ for bidirectional enterprise integration. Budget separately from the MuleSoft licence itself.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">04</span><h3>Usage-based overage</h3><p>Tableau impressions, MuleSoft messages and flows, Data Cloud credits and Archive storage all bill <strong>monthly in arrears</strong>. They will not be on your annual PO and they are the most common budget surprise we see.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">05</span><h3>Premier Success</h3><p>30% of net licence fees on legacy editions. On a $100K contract that is $30K a year. Already bundled into the new Core / Advanced / Max tiers, make sure it is not quoted twice.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">06</span><h3>Sandboxes</h3><p>Priced as a percentage of net spend, not a flat fee. Full Copy is 30%. On a $100K contract that is $30K a year for an environment you may genuinely need for only two months of UAT.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">07</span><h3>Change orders</h3><p>Plan for <strong>10&ndash;30%</strong> of original scope. Requirements move once users see the first build. A partner who tells you otherwise is pricing to win, not to deliver.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">08</span><h3>Ongoing administration</h3><p>$650&ndash;$1,000/month for a block of hours, $1,800&ndash;$3,000 for a fractional admin, $7,000&ndash;$12,000 for a dedicated one. The system does not maintain itself after go-live.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">09</span><h3>Renewal uplift</h3><p>We have measured 5.25%, 5.26%, 7.84% and 9.0% on real renewals. Compounded over three years that is a material number. Cap it in writing at signature.</p></div>

            <div class="imp-ce-wrap">
                <div class="imp-ce-center">
                    <span class="imp-ce-kicker">From The Contracts</span>
                    <h2 class="imp-ce-h2">Nine things we learned reading 94 order forms</h2>
                    <p class="imp-ce-sub">Specific, checkable, and drawn from signed paper rather than from a vendor blog.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="imp-ce-cols">
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 01</span><h3>Ask for the nonprofit rate even if you think you don't qualify</h3><p>Our data shows the identical SKU, Sales Cloud Enterprise Edition, at <strong>$36.00</strong> on one contract and <strong>$114.96</strong> on another. Status, not volume, drove the difference.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 02</span><h3>Never buy add-ons mid-term at a full-year price</h3><p>Every contract we hold co-terms additions to a single anniversary. We see fractional terms of 7.17, 8.90, 9.47, 10.17, 11.43 and 22.93 months. Make them show you the stub period.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 03</span><h3>Cap the renewal uplift in writing</h3><p>Not at renewal, at signature. A 3&ndash;5% cap is achievable. Without one you are exposed to the 7&ndash;9% we have seen applied.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 04</span><h3>Don't pay for Premier twice</h3><p>On legacy editions it is 30% of net licences. On Core, Advanced and Max it is bundled. We have seen quotes that add it back on top regardless.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 05</span><h3>Portal licence type is the cliff nobody mentions</h3><p>Legacy nonprofit community rates on our contracts run <strong>$0.32 to $1.25 per login</strong>. Today&rsquo;s nonprofit list is <strong>$0.50 per login</strong> for Customer Community, but $1.50 for Customer Community Plus and $2.50 for Partner Community, and commercial rates are four times higher. Give each audience the lightest licence it needs</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 06</span><h3>Usage lines bill monthly in arrears</h3><p>Tableau impressions at $0.469 each, MuleSoft messages at $0.00099, Data Cloud credits, Archive GB. Your annual budget will not have them in it.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 07</span><h3>MuleSoft's entry price is not a real price</h3><p>"From $2,000/month" sits behind a <strong>$50,000 minimum annual commitment</strong>. Price the integration work against iPaaS alternatives before you commit.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 08</span><h3>Zero-dollar lines are entitlements, not discounts</h3><p>Data Cloud Provisioning, Salesforce Foundations, Tableau Next Provisioning, Force.com Administrator, all appear at $0.00. Nobody gave you anything.</p></div>
                    <div class="imp-ce-icard"><span class="imp-num">TIP 09</span><h3>Ask what happens to NPSP</h3><p>The Nonprofit Success Pack has had no new features since March 2023. If a partner is still proposing a fresh NPSP build in 2026, ask them to justify it against Nonprofit Cloud.</p></div>
                </div>

                    <details><summary>Can I use a Salesforce cost estimator if I know nothing about Salesforce?</summary><p>That is exactly who it is built for. Every question is about your organisation, not about Salesforce, how many staff, what you want the system to do, whether outsiders need to log in, where your data lives today. The tool translates those answers into clouds, editions and licences, and explains each choice in plain English. You never have to pick a product name.</p></details>
                    <details><summary>How accurate is the TwinStack Salesforce cost estimator?</summary><p>It is a well-informed starting point, not a scoping exercise. A few questions cannot capture everything, so treat the output as a budget bracket and a shortlist to challenge your vendor with. The licence arithmetic is solid; the implementation figure is a range because scope is what drives it and we have not seen yours.</p></details>
                    <details><summary>Where do the Salesforce benchmark prices in the estimator come from?</summary><p>From 94 order forms and invoices across 19 Salesforce contracts that TwinStack administers, covering 2021 to 2027 and ranging from $0 to $158,642 in annual value. They are anonymised and aggregated. No client is identifiable from anything on this page.</p></details>
                    <details><summary>What changed in Salesforce pricing in September 2026?</summary><p>On 3 September 2026 Salesforce retired Enterprise, Unlimited, Einstein 1 and Agentforce 1 from its price list and replaced them with Core ($195), Advanced ($395) and Max ($550). All three bundle Slack, Tableau Next, Premier Success, enterprise security and Agentforce, plus an annual allocation of Flex Credits. Existing contracts are grandfathered at their current pricing.</p></details>
                    <details><summary>Do nonprofits really get 10 free Salesforce licences?</summary><p>If you are a registered nonprofit, yes. The Power of Us programme grants 10 Nonprofit Cloud Core licences at no cost, and it appears on 9 of the 19 contracts we analysed. Premier Success activates once you make your first paid purchase. Educational institutions are also eligible. We will run the application for you at no charge.</p></details>
                    <details><summary>Why does Salesforce implementation cost more than the software?</summary><p>Because the licence buys you an empty platform. The cost is in the data model, the migration, the integrations, the permission architecture and the change management. The industry benchmark is 1.5 to 3 times annual licence spend, and in our experience the projects that come in under that are the ones that scoped ruthlessly rather than the ones that found a cheaper partner.</p></details>

That is deliberate, it is why the tool is willing to tell you that you do not need Tableau or MuleSoft.</p></details>

We do not pass it to Salesforce or to any third party.</p></details>
