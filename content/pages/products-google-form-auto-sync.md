<section style="background:#FDF1EF"><div class="imp-wrap"><div class="imp-sec-head imp-rv"><div class="imp-idx"><b>01</b> The problem</div>
<h2>Google Forms are easy to build. Getting the data out is not.</h2><p class="imp-lead">Sales, HR, operations, marketing and support all reach for Google Forms because they take minutes to make. Then the response lands in a spreadsheet, disconnected from the CRM where the business actually runs.</p></div>
<div class="imp-g3"><div class="imp-card imp-rv"><div class="imp-xm">&#10005;</div><h3>Manual export and import</h3><p>Someone downloads a CSV, cleans it, and uploads it to Salesforce. Slow, error-prone, and very easy to forget on a busy week.</p></div><div class="imp-card imp-rv"><div class="imp-xm">&#10005;</div><h3>Paid integration middleware</h3><p>Platforms that charge per task or per run, and add another vendor, another login, and another point of failure to your stack.</p></div><div class="imp-card imp-rv"><div class="imp-xm">&#10005;</div><h3>Custom-built integrations</h3><p>Internal development effort that takes time to build, and considerably more time to maintain once whoever wrote it moves on.</p></div></div><p class="imp-pull imp-rv">Every hour a response sits outside Salesforce is an hour a rep is not following up, a hiring manager is not reviewing an application, or an organiser cannot see a new registration.</p></div></section>
<section id="what" style="background:#EDF3FD"><div class="imp-wrap">
<div class="imp-sec-head imp-rv"><div class="imp-idx"><b>02</b> What it is</div><h2>The simplest possible bridge between Google Forms and Salesforce.</h2><p class="imp-lead">A lightweight Google Apps Script that lives inside your form, paired with a REST endpoint inside your Salesforce org. A submission goes in one side, a structured record comes out the other.</p></div>
<div class="imp-flow imp-rv"><div class="imp-node"><div class="imp-node-ic">&#9671;</div><h3>Your Google Form</h3><p>Apps Script fires the instant someone hits submit. Included in the package, pasted in once per form.</p></div>
<div class="imp-lnk"><div class="imp-lnk-l"></div><span class="imp-lnk-t">OAuth 2.0</span><span class="imp-lnk-t">Direct REST call</span></div>
<div class="imp-node"><div class="imp-node-ic">&#9737;</div><h3>Your Salesforce org</h3><p>Creates a <code>Form_Response__c</code> record with the form name, ID, timestamp and full response.</p></div></div>
<span class="imp-flow-note imp-rv">Responses travel between two parties only: Google, where the form lives, and your Salesforce org. No TwinStack servers or other middleware sit in between.</span>
<p class="imp-lead imp-rv" style="margin:50px auto 0;text-align:center;max-width:72ch">It solves the capture problem, deliberately, rather than trying to replace your data model. Every submission is visible, searchable and reportable in Salesforce, ready to route with Flow, list views and reports you already know.</p></div></section>

<section id="faq" style="background:#FEF8E7"><div class="imp-wrap"><div class="imp-sec-head imp-rv"><div class="imp-idx"><b>10</b> FAQ</div>
<h2>The questions admins ask first.</h2></div><div class="imp-faq-list"><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Is there a free Google Forms to Salesforce integration?</summary><p>Yes. Google Form Auto Sync is a free, native Salesforce app on AppExchange. It sends every Google Form response into Salesforce in real time, with no licence fee, no per-response charges and no middleware.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>What is the purpose of Google Form Auto Sync?</summary><p>To eliminate the manual work of getting Google Form responses into Salesforce. It gives teams already using Google Forms a way to capture every submission in their CRM automatically, in real time, without exports, imports, or a paid integration platform.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>How does Google Form Auto Sync work?</summary><p>A small Google Apps Script, included in the package and set up through a guided wizard, runs inside your Google Form. When someone submits, the script securely sends the response to a Salesforce REST endpoint, which creates a record the moment it arrives, typically within seconds.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Can Google Form responses create Leads or Contacts in Salesforce?</summary><p>Each response is first saved as a Form Response record, so nothing is lost. You then use a record-triggered Flow or assignment rules to create or update Leads, Contacts, Cases or Campaign Members.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Does Google Form Auto Sync work with Nonprofit Cloud and NPSP?</summary><p>Yes. It works in any Salesforce org, including Nonprofit Cloud and NPSP. Use Flow to route responses to Contacts, Program Enrollments (Nonprofit Cloud), Program Engagements (NPSP with the Program Management Module), Cases or Campaign Members.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>How much does Google Form Auto Sync cost?</summary><p>Nothing. It is a free managed package with no licence fee, no per-response charge, and no tiered pricing.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Is Google Form Auto Sync secure?</summary><p>Responses travel between two parties only: Google, where the form lives, and your Salesforce org. No TwinStack servers or other middleware sit in between. Authentication uses the OAuth 2.0 Client Credentials Flow against a Salesforce External Client App, requests run under a single dedicated Run-As user, and every record insert enforces field-level security through Salesforce's insert-as-user model.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>What happens if Salesforce is unavailable when someone submits a Google Form?</summary><p>If Salesforce is unavailable when someone submits, the response is still saved in Google Forms, but it isn't sent again automatically. Check the dashboard's last-sync time to spot any gaps.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Do I need a developer to set up Google Form Auto Sync?</summary><p>No. The in-app 5-step wizard is built for Salesforce admins and includes copy-ready code and credential steps. Setup takes about 15 minutes for your first form and about 5 minutes for each one after that. If you route responses with a Flow, allow extra time to build it.</p></details></div></div></section>
