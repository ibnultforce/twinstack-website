    <section class="imp-bg-white imp-relative"><div class="imp-lg-wrap"><article>
<p class="imp-text-xl imp-text-slate-600 imp-font-medium imp-mb-8">TwinStack Solutions builds AppExchange apps that run inside your own Salesforce org. This page explains how our apps handle your data, the security practices we follow, and how to reach us about security.</p>
<nav class="imp-lg-toc" aria-label="On this page"><b>On this page</b><ol><li><a href="#commitments">Our commitments</a></li><li><a href="#apps">How each app handles data</a></li><li><a href="#practices">Security practices</a></li><li><a href="#shared">Shared responsibility</a></li><li><a href="#receive">What we do receive</a></li><li><a href="#website">Our website</a></li><li><a href="#report">Reporting a security issue</a></li><li><a href="#documents">Related documents</a></li><li><a href="#contact">Contact</a></li></ol></nav>
<h2 id="commitments">Our commitments</h2>
<div class="imp-lg-grid"><div class="imp-lg-card"><b>Reviewed by Salesforce.</b><span>Every app we publish on AppExchange has passed Salesforce's security review.</span></div><div class="imp-lg-card"><b>Runs in your org.</b><span>Our apps are native, built with Apex and Lightning Web Components, and run inside your Salesforce org.</span></div><div class="imp-lg-card"><b>No TwinStack servers in the path.</b><span>Your data is never routed through or stored on TwinStack systems.</span></div><div class="imp-lg-card"><b>We don't access your data.</b><span>We can't see your Salesforce records, form responses or credentials.</span></div></div>
<h2 id="apps">How each app handles data</h2>
<div class="imp-article-table-wrap"><table><thead><tr><th>App</th><th>Where it runs</th><th>External connections</th><th>Data TwinStack receives</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><a href="/products/google-form-auto-sync.html">Google Form Auto Sync</a></td><td>Your Salesforce org, plus a script inside your own Google Form</td><td>Your Google Form sends responses directly to your org</td><td>None</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/products/who-sees-what.html">Who Sees What</a></td><td>Your Salesforce org (read-only)</td><td>None</td><td>None</td></tr><tr><td><a href="/products/smart-forms-surveys.html">Smart Forms &amp; Surveys</a></td><td>Your Salesforce org, plus a public Salesforce Site you control</td><td>None. Visitors submit through your own Salesforce Site</td><td>None</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p><a href="/products/smart-lookup-data-loader.html">Smart Lookup Data Loader</a> also runs inside your Salesforce org, and TwinStack receives none of its data.</p><p>"Data TwinStack receives" refers to your Salesforce records and app data. Salesforce shares standard installation information with all AppExchange partners. See <a href="#receive">What we do receive</a> below.</p>
<h2 id="practices">Security practices</h2>
<ul><li><strong>Salesforce security review:</strong> each app passes Salesforce's review before it is listed on AppExchange. You can see the status on each listing's Security tab.</li><li><strong>Salesforce permissions respected:</strong> our apps enforce field-level security and object permissions, so users only see and change what Salesforce allows them to.</li><li><strong>Least privilege:</strong> apps ship with permission sets that grant only what's needed. Your admins decide who gets them.</li><li><strong>Secure authentication:</strong> Google Form Auto Sync uses Salesforce's OAuth 2.0 Client Credentials flow through an External Client App, with a dedicated user you choose. No passwords are stored.</li><li><strong>Managed packages:</strong> apps are delivered as Salesforce managed packages, so updates and security fixes reach you through Salesforce's standard upgrade process.</li></ul>
<h2 id="shared">Shared responsibility</h2>
<p>Because our apps run in your org, some controls are yours:</p><ul><li>Decide which users get each app's permission sets.</li><li>For Google Form Auto Sync, limit edit access to connected Google Forms. The connection's Consumer Key and Secret are stored in the form's script properties, so form editors can see them.</li><li>Choose and manage the integration user that creates records.</li><li>Keep your org's sharing rules and field-level security set the way you need them.</li></ul>
<h2 id="receive">What we do receive</h2>
<p>When you install one of our apps, Salesforce shares standard installation information with us: your org ID, Salesforce edition, package version, and the installing user's name, email and company. We use it to provide support and send important updates. Read our <a href="/privacy-policy.html">Privacy Policy</a> for details.</p>
<h2 id="website">Our website</h2>
<p>Our website is served over HTTPS through Cloudflare. Enquiries from our contact forms and the <a href="/cost-estimator.html">Salesforce Cost Estimator</a> are stored in our own Salesforce org. The website assistant chat sends your messages through our Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic's API to generate a reply, and we do not store chat conversations. Images are delivered by Cloudinary. We measure website visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics, and Google Analytics cookies are only set if you accept them in our cookie banner. Our apps do not send your data to any of these providers.</p>
<h2 id="report">Reporting a security issue</h2>
<p>If you think you've found a security vulnerability in one of our apps, email <a href="mailto:info@twinstack.net?subject=Security">info@twinstack.net</a> with the subject line "Security". Please include the app name, version and steps to reproduce. We acknowledge reports within 2 business days and keep you updated until the issue is resolved. Please don't share details publicly until we've fixed it.</p>
