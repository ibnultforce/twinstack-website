<section class="imp-sv-hero"><div class="imp-w imp-g"><div><h1>Simple Salesforce pricing</h1><p class="imp-lead">One standard rate: US$30 an hour, or A$47 for clients in Australia. Pay as you go, or agree a monthly hours plan and scale it up when you need more.</p><div class="imp-ctas"><a class="imp-btn imp-bp" href="https://calendar.app.google/HP3LiuNFisCt6xLo6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Plan your custom pricing</a></div></div><aside class="imp-sv-facts" aria-label="Rates"><dl><div><dt>Standard rate</dt><dd>US$30 an hour</dd></div><div><dt>Australia</dt><dd>A$47 an hour</dd></div><div><dt>Billing</dt><dd>Monthly, on hours delivered</dd></div><div><dt>First call</dt><dd>Free, 30 minutes</dd></div></dl></aside></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="models"><div class="imp-w"><h2>Ways to work with us</h2><p class="imp-intro">Every option uses the same hourly rate, and you only pay for hours we deliver.</p><div class="imp-pr-models"><div><h3>Monthly hours plan</h3><p class="imp-pc">A baseline you agree</p><p>A set number of hours each month at US$30 / A$47 an hour, usually over 12 months. We plan the work with you every month and scale up in heavier periods when agreed in advance.</p><p class="imp-bf">Best for: ongoing support, enhancements and long-running programmes</p></div><div><h3>Pay as you go</h3><p class="imp-pc">US$30 / A$47 an hour</p><p>No commitment. We agree the task, do the work and bill the hours it took.</p><p class="imp-bf">Best for: one-off fixes, advice and small changes</p></div><div><h3>Projects</h3><p class="imp-pc">Estimated in hours</p><p>Implementations, migrations and AppExchange builds are estimated in hours at our standard rate, line by line, so you can see what each part costs.</p><p class="imp-bf">Best for: new builds with a defined scope</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec imp-tint" id="plan"><div class="imp-w"><h2>A monthly hours plan, worked out</h2><p class="imp-intro">An example plan with a 30-hour baseline. Your baseline is agreed with you, and you are billed monthly on the hours actually delivered.</p><div style="overflow-x:auto;margin-top:2rem"><table class="imp-pr-tbl" style="margin-top:0"><thead><tr><th>Month</th><th>Hours</th><th>When</th><th>US$</th><th>A$</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Baseline month</td><td>30 hours</td><td>The agreed monthly commitment</td><td>US$900</td><td>A$1,410</td></tr><tr><td>Busier month</td><td>60 hours</td><td>Agreed with you in advance</td><td>US$1,800</td><td>A$2,820</td></tr><tr><td>Peak month</td><td>70 hours</td><td>Agreed with you in advance</td><td>US$2,100</td><td>A$3,290</td></tr><tr><td><b>12 months at baseline</b></td><td>360 hours</td><td>Over the year</td><td><b>US$10,800</b></td><td><b>A$16,920</b></td></tr></tbody></table></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="how"><div class="imp-w"><h2>How a monthly plan runs</h2><div class="imp-sv-why"><div><h3>Monthly planning</h3><p>At the start of each month we agree the priorities with you and plan the hours against them.</p></div><div><h3>Transparent hour tracking</h3><p>Hours are tracked against each piece of work and reported to you every month.</p></div><div><h3>One point of contact</h3><p>Our Salesforce Architect is your single point of contact, with designers and developers joining as the work needs.</p></div><div><h3>Flexible capacity</h3><p>Scale up for busier periods, such as a release or a migration, when agreed in advance. Extra hours are at the same rate.</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec imp-tint" id="included"><div class="imp-w"><h2>What you get, and what is extra</h2><div class="imp-sv-why"><div><h3>Included in our rate</h3><p>Architects, developers, QA engineers and project managers, plus documentation and handover.</p></div><div><h3>Paid separately</h3><p>Salesforce licences and any third-party apps, which you pay to their vendors. Our free AppExchange apps cost nothing.</p></div><div><h3>A plan built around you</h3><p>Tell us about your workload and we will suggest a monthly baseline that fits. <a href="/contact.html">Call us for a custom plan</a>.</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="imp-sec" id="faq"><div class="imp-w"><h2>Pricing questions</h2><div class="imp-sv-faq"><details><summary>Is the first call free?</summary><p>Yes. The first 30-minute call is free. We look at what you need and tell you how we would approach it before any paid work starts.</p></details><details><summary>How does a monthly hours plan work?</summary><p>We agree a baseline number of hours a month with you, usually over a 12-month term. Each month we plan the work together, and you are billed for the hours we deliver at US$30 (A$47) an hour.</p></details><details><summary>What if we need more hours in a month?</summary><p>Tell us ahead of a busy period and we scale up. Additional hours are billed at the same rate, only when you have approved them.</p></details><details><summary>How do we know where the hours went?</summary><p>We track hours against each piece of work and report them to you every month, so every invoice can be checked against the work delivered.</p></details><details><summary>Are Salesforce licences included?</summary><p>No. Salesforce licences and any third-party apps are paid to their vendors, separately from our hours.</p></details><details><summary>Why is there a separate Australian dollar rate?</summary><p>We bill Australian clients in Australian dollars at A$47 an hour, so there are no currency conversions on your invoice.</p></details><details><summary>How do I get an estimate for a project?</summary><p>Call us and tell us what you need. We estimate projects in hours at our standard rate, line by line, so you can check every part before work starts.</p></details></div></div></section>
