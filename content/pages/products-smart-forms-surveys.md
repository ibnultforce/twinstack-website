<section style="background:#FDF1EF"><div class="imp-wrap"><div class="imp-sec-head imp-rv"><div class="imp-idx"><b>01</b> The problem</div>
<h2>Collecting data from outside Salesforce should not need another tool.</h2><p class="imp-lead">Registrations, applications, feedback and enquiries all start with someone outside your org filling in a form. Getting those answers into the right Salesforce record is where most teams end up compromising.</p></div>
<div class="imp-g3"><div class="imp-card imp-rv"><div class="imp-xm">&#10005;</div><h3 class="imp-h4s">Web-to-Lead and Web-to-Case</h3><p>Free and built in, but limited to Leads or Cases, with basic generated HTML and no builder for surveys or multi-step forms.</p></div><div class="imp-card imp-rv"><div class="imp-xm">&#10005;</div><h3 class="imp-h4s">Third-party form builders</h3><p>Polished builders, but usually paid, and every submission passes through the vendor's servers before it reaches Salesforce.</p></div><div class="imp-card imp-rv"><div class="imp-xm">&#10005;</div><h3 class="imp-h4s">Custom-coded forms</h3><p>A developer builds a Visualforce or Lightning page for each form, and every new question becomes another change request.</p></div></div><p class="imp-pull imp-rv">The result is forms that are hard to change, data that travels further than it needs to, and answers that still have to be copied into the record where they belong.</p></div></section>
<section id="what" style="background:#EDF3FD"><div class="imp-wrap">
<div class="imp-sec-head imp-rv"><div class="imp-idx"><b>02</b> What it is</div><h2>A form builder that lives inside your Salesforce org.</h2><p class="imp-lead">You design the form in Salesforce, publish it on a Salesforce Site, and share the public link. When someone submits, the record is created or updated in your org. The form, the submission and the data never leave the platform.</p></div>
<div class="imp-flow imp-rv"><div class="imp-node"><div class="imp-node-ic">&#9998;</div><h3>Design and publish</h3><p>Build the form in the drag-and-drop Form Builder, choose the object it writes to, and publish it to a Salesforce Site.</p></div>
<div class="imp-lnk"><div class="imp-lnk-l"></div><span class="imp-lnk-t">Public link</span><span class="imp-lnk-t">Native submission</span></div>
<div class="imp-node"><div class="imp-node-ic">&#9737;</div><h3>Record in your org</h3><p>Each submission creates or updates a record on any standard or custom object, under the security rules you set.</p></div></div>
<span class="imp-flow-note imp-rv">Everything runs natively on the Salesforce platform: no external callouts, no OAuth tokens and no third-party servers.</span>
</div></section>

<h2>The questions admins ask first.</h2></div><div class="imp-faq-list"><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Is there a free form builder for Salesforce?</summary><p>Yes. Smart Forms &amp; Surveys is a free, native app on the Salesforce AppExchange. It never requires payment, and it lets admins build public web forms and surveys by ticking fields in a no-code builder.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Do people need a Salesforce login to fill in a form?</summary><p>No. Forms are published on a Salesforce Site, so anonymous visitors can open the public link and submit without logging in. Guest access runs through a dedicated least-privilege permission set.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Which Salesforce objects can a form write to?</summary><p>Any standard or custom object. A submission can create a new record or update an existing one, so the same tool works for Leads, Contacts, Cases, custom application objects and more.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>How long does setup take?</summary><p>About 10 minutes, once. A built-in guide walks you through five steps: builder access, public site, visitor access, sharing rule and link address.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Can I show a question only for certain answers?</summary><p>Yes. Make a question conditional on the answer to another question, for example show Partner Name only when Lead Source is Partner Referral. You can test the rule in the builder, and a hidden answer is cleared so it never reaches the record.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Can I make a field required on one form only?</summary><p>Yes. You can rename a question, add placeholder text and make it required for a single form. The field on your object stays unchanged.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Can I preview a form before I share it?</summary><p>Yes. Switch to Visitor view in the builder to see the form exactly as visitors will. Required fields are checked and dependent questions appear live.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>How do I see what happened to each submission?</summary><p>The Submissions list shows every response, the record it created and what the visitor sent. If a submission failed, you can see what went wrong.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Does any form data leave Salesforce?</summary><p>No. Smart Forms &amp; Surveys runs entirely on the Salesforce platform. There are no external callouts, no OAuth tokens and no third-party servers, so submissions go straight from your Salesforce Site into your org.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>How are guest submissions kept secure?</summary><p>Guest access runs through a dedicated least-privilege permission set. Submissions respect object and field-level security, and the app runs in user mode with sharing enforced, so a guest can only write what you have allowed.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Do I need a developer to set it up?</summary><p>No. Forms are built by ticking fields in a no-code builder. A built-in guide walks you through a one-time, five-step setup of about 10 minutes, and a site checker confirms your public site is ready. A Salesforce admin can do the whole setup.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>What can I use Smart Forms &amp; Surveys for?</summary><p>Lead capture, event registration, surveys, feedback, intake and application forms, and any other form where the answers belong in Salesforce.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>Should I use Smart Forms &amp; Surveys or Google Form Auto Sync?</summary><p>If your team already builds forms in Google Forms and wants the responses in Salesforce, use Google Form Auto Sync. If you want the form itself to live on Salesforce, built and hosted in your own org with no Google account involved, use Smart Forms &amp; Surveys. Both are free.</p></details><details class="imp-rv"><summary>How much does Smart Forms &amp; Surveys cost?</summary><p>Nothing. It is listed as free on AppExchange and never requires payment.</p></details></div>
