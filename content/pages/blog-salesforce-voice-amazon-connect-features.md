                <p class="imp-text-xl imp-text-slate-600 imp-font-medium imp-mb-8"><strong>Short answer:</strong> Salesforce Voice (formerly Service Cloud Voice) puts Amazon Connect telephony inside the Salesforce Service Console. Agents answer, transcribe, record and log calls in one screen, and every call becomes a Salesforce record that automation, AI and reports can use.</p>
<p>Digital channels have grown fast, but voice is still the highest-stakes touchpoint for complex customer questions. Older set-ups split telephony and CRM into separate systems, which created bottlenecks and kept native AI away from live calls.</p>
<p>Salesforce Voice with Amazon Connect replaces that model. Built on AWS infrastructure, it brings live audio, real-time transcription, CRM automation and Salesforce AI into a single screen.</p>
<h2 id="how-the-data-flows-between-amazon-connect-and-salesforce">How the data flows between Amazon Connect and Salesforce</h2>
<p>The integration runs in both directions. Amazon Connect handles the call itself, while Salesforce handles routing, records and the agent's workspace.</p>

<h2 id="core-salesforce-voice-features">Core Salesforce Voice features</h2>
<h3 id="dynamic-phone-menus-with-personalisation">Dynamic phone menus with personalisation</h3>
<p>Traditional phone menus follow rigid, static trees. Salesforce Voice can build menus that respond to who is calling.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Caller identification:</strong> finds the matching customer record from the phone number.</li>
<li><strong>Context-driven options:</strong> if the caller has an open high-priority case, the menu can offer an update on it.</li>
<li><strong>Self-service:</strong> natural language bots can answer common questions without an agent.</li>
<li><strong>Unified routing:</strong> calls follow the same routing logic and queue rules as chat, messaging and email.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="customer-360-view-during-the-call">Customer 360 view during the call</h3>
<p>When a call reaches an agent, the customer's record opens on screen, so nobody has to search for it.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Instant screen pop:</strong> the moment the agent accepts, Salesforce opens the matched customer record.</li>
<li><strong>VoiceCall record:</strong> every call creates a dedicated record with the call details, such as duration, and a link to the recording.</li>
<li><strong>One console:</strong> during the call, the agent sees the customer's history, past cases and recent activity in one place.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="real-time-transcription-and-sentiment-analysis">Real-time transcription and sentiment analysis</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Live transcript:</strong> words appear in the console as they are spoken, so agents do not need to take notes by hand.</li>
<li><strong>Sentiment analysis:</strong> the conversation is analysed as it happens, showing shifts between positive, neutral and negative.</li>
<li><strong>Automatic redaction:</strong> sensitive details such as card numbers and national ID numbers can be removed from transcripts.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="call-recording-and-compliance">Call recording and compliance</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Built-in audio player:</strong> recordings play next to the transcript, with variable speed and a visual waveform.</li>
<li><strong>Pause and resume:</strong> agents can pause recording while collecting sensitive data, and recording can pause automatically during payments.</li>
</ul>

Call recording player with a visual waveform (illustration). <span>Click to enlarge.</span></figcaption></figure>
<h3 id="voicemail-management">Voicemail management</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Inbound voicemail:</strong> voicemails are transcribed and routed to the right agent or queue, with the audio and full transcript on the record.</li>
<li><strong>Voicemail drop:</strong> agents can leave a pre-recorded message on a customer's answering machine with one click.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="post-call-surveys">Post-call surveys</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Straight after the call:</strong> the caller moves to a short survey as soon as the agent hangs up.</li>
<li><strong>Closed-loop feedback:</strong> scores land on the customer record, and a low score can create a follow-up task for a supervisor.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="agentforce-and-einstein-ai">Agentforce and Einstein AI</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Voice agents:</strong> Agentforce can hold conversations, take actions and hand over to a human agent with the full context.</li>
<li><strong>Next best action:</strong> keywords in the conversation can trigger recommendations, such as an approved offer or a retention script.</li>
<li><strong>Call summaries:</strong> generative AI can write a standard summary at the end of the call (reason, key points, resolution, next steps), which cuts wrap-up time.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="crm-automation-and-wrap-up-controls">CRM automation and wrap-up controls</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Automatic records:</strong> Flows can create cases and log activities without manual data entry.</li>
<li><strong>Presence sync:</strong> the agent's phone status stays in step with Omni-Channel, so other work such as chats pauses during a call.</li>
<li><strong>Wrap-up timers:</strong> admins set how long agents get for after-call work before the next call arrives.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="reporting-dashboards-and-supervisor-tools">Reporting, dashboards and supervisor tools</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Real-time view:</strong> supervisors see active agents, queue loads, live call times and sentiment across calls.</li>
<li><strong>Monitor, whisper and barge:</strong> supervisors can listen in, speak only to the agent, or join the call.</li>
<li><strong>Historical reporting:</strong> voice metrics sit next to standard Salesforce data, so dashboards can show average handle time, first contact resolution and other contact centre KPIs.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3 class="imp-q">What is Salesforce Voice?</h3>
<p>Salesforce Voice, formerly Service Cloud Voice, is Salesforce's contact centre telephony. It brings calls, transcripts and recordings into the Service Console, using Amazon Connect or a partner telephony provider.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Does Salesforce Voice transcribe calls in real time?</h3>
<p>Yes. With Amazon Connect, the transcript appears in the console as people speak, and sensitive details can be redacted.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Is every call saved in Salesforce?</h3>
<p>Each call creates a VoiceCall record with call details and links to the recording and transcript, so automation and reports can use it.</p>
<h3 class="imp-q">Can Agentforce work with Salesforce Voice?</h3>
<p>Yes. Agentforce can handle voice conversations and hand over to a human agent with context, and generative AI can summarise calls.</p>
