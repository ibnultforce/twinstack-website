About Saiful

Saiful Islam is a Salesforce System Architect and the technical lead at TwinStack Solutions, a Salesforce consultancy and AppExchange PDO based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Over more than a decade in the Salesforce ecosystem, he has guided projects from feasibility and architecture through development, security review and AppExchange listing.

His focus is the full ISV lifecycle: validating ideas against Salesforce governor limits, designing data models that scale from ten to ten thousand users, and shipping native applications built on Lightning Web Components and Apex. He leads a team of 35 people serving clients across North America, Europe and Australia.

He founded Twinforce Solutions in August 2022 and has been Managing Director of Twinforce Solutions Limited, the parent company of TwinStack, since January 2024.
