01 The problem

Collecting data from outside Salesforce should not need another tool.

Registrations, applications, feedback and enquiries all start with someone outside your org filling in a form. Getting those answers into the right Salesforce record is where most teams end up compromising.

✕

Web-to-Lead and Web-to-Case

Free and built in, but limited to Leads or Cases, with basic generated HTML and no builder for surveys or multi-step forms.

✕

Third-party form builders

Polished builders, but usually paid, and every submission passes through the vendor's servers before it reaches Salesforce.

✕

Custom-coded forms

A developer builds a Visualforce or Lightning page for each form, and every new question becomes another change request.

The result is forms that are hard to change, data that travels further than it needs to, and answers that still have to be copied into the record where they belong.

02 What it is

A form builder that lives inside your Salesforce org.

You design the form in Salesforce, publish it on a Salesforce Site, and share the public link. When someone submits, the record is created or updated in your org. The form, the submission and the data never leave the platform.

✎

Design and publish

Build the form in the drag-and-drop Form Builder, choose the object it writes to, and publish it to a Salesforce Site.

03 How it works

From install to your first live form.

Set up the public site once, then build and publish as many forms as you need from the same builder.

01

Install the package

Install Smart Forms & Surveys from AppExchange. It adds the Forms, Form Submissions, Form Builder and Form Configuration tabs to your org.

02

Check your public site

Open the Form Configuration tab. Its site checker confirms your public form site is set up and active, and the built-in instructions walk you through anything missing.

03

Grant guest access

Guest visitors submit through a dedicated least-privilege permission set, so you decide exactly which objects and fields a public form can write to.

04

Design the form

Drag questions into the Form Builder and choose the standard or custom object each submission should create or update. No code required.

05

Publish and share

Publish the form to your Salesforce Site and share its public link on your website, in an email or in a QR code.

06

Submissions become records

Anonymous visitors fill in the form, and the record is created or updated in your org, ready for your Flows, reports and list views.

10 FAQ

The questions admins ask first.

Is there a free form builder for Salesforce?

Yes. Smart Forms & Surveys is a free, native app on the Salesforce AppExchange. It never requires payment, and it lets admins build public web forms and surveys with a drag-and-drop builder, with no code.

Do people need a Salesforce login to fill in a form?

No. Forms are published on a Salesforce Site, so anonymous visitors can open the public link and submit without logging in. Guest access runs through a dedicated least-privilege permission set.

Which Salesforce objects can a form write to?

Any standard or custom object. A submission can create a new record or update an existing one, so the same tool works for Leads, Contacts, Cases, custom application objects and more.

Does any form data leave Salesforce?

No. Smart Forms & Surveys runs entirely on the Salesforce platform. There are no external callouts, no OAuth tokens and no third-party servers, so submissions go straight from your Salesforce Site into your org.

How are guest submissions kept secure?

Guest access runs through a dedicated least-privilege permission set. Submissions respect object and field-level security, and the app runs in user mode with sharing enforced, so a guest can only write what you have allowed.

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. Forms are designed in a drag-and-drop builder, and the Form Configuration tab checks that your public site is ready and shows step-by-step instructions for anything that is missing. A Salesforce admin can do the whole setup.

What can I use Smart Forms & Surveys for?

Lead capture, event registration, surveys, feedback, intake and application forms, and any other form where the answers belong in Salesforce.

Should I use Smart Forms & Surveys or Google Form Auto Sync?

If your team already builds forms in Google Forms and wants the responses in Salesforce, use Google Form Auto Sync. If you want the form itself to live on Salesforce, built and hosted in your own org with no Google account involved, use Smart Forms & Surveys. Both are free.

How much does Smart Forms & Surveys cost?

Nothing. It is listed as free on AppExchange and never requires payment.
