01 The problem

Google Forms are easy to build. Getting the data out is not.

Sales, HR, operations, marketing and support all reach for Google Forms because they take minutes to make. Then the response lands in a spreadsheet, disconnected from the CRM where the business actually runs.

✕

Manual export and import

Someone downloads a CSV, cleans it, and uploads it to Salesforce. Slow, error-prone, and very easy to forget on a busy week.

✕

Paid integration middleware

Platforms that charge per task or per run, and add another vendor, another login, and another point of failure to your stack.

✕

Custom-built integrations

Internal development effort that takes time to build, and considerably more time to maintain once whoever wrote it moves on.

Every hour a response sits outside Salesforce is an hour a rep is not following up, a hiring manager is not reviewing an application, or an organiser cannot see a new registration.

02 What it is

The simplest possible bridge between Google Forms and Salesforce.

A lightweight Google Apps Script that lives inside your form, paired with a REST endpoint inside your Salesforce org. A submission goes in one side, a structured record comes out the other.

◇

Your Google Form

Apps Script fires the instant someone hits submit. Included in the package, pasted in once per form.

OAuth 2.0Direct REST call
☉

Your Salesforce org

Creates a Form_Response__c record with the form name, ID, timestamp and full response.

Responses travel between two parties only: Google, where the form lives, and your Salesforce org. No TwinStack servers or other middleware sit in between.

It solves the capture problem, deliberately, rather than trying to replace your data model. Every submission is visible, searchable and reportable in Salesforce, ready to route with Flow, list views and reports you already know.

03 How it works

Six steps to set up. Zero steps after that.

Install the package, then follow the 5-step setup wizard for each form. The guided wizard walks an admin through it end to end, with copy-ready code and credentials at each stage.

01

Install the package

The managed package installs from AppExchange in a few clicks and sets up the object, permission sets, and a guided setup tab.

02

Connect once, per form

Paste the included Apps Script into your form's script editor. The 5-step wizard walks you through it and generates what the script needs.

03

Authenticate securely

OAuth 2.0 Client Credentials Flow against a Salesforce External Client App. No passwords stored, access scoped to one Run-As user.

04

Submit and sync

Someone submits the form, the script fires automatically, packages the response, and sends it to a purpose-built Salesforce REST endpoint.

05

Record created instantly

Salesforce enforces field-level security through an insert-as-user operation and creates the record, typically within seconds of submission.

06

View and act

It appears on the built-in dashboard and on the record itself, where the viewer parses the submission into readable question-and-answer pairs.

06 Security

No third-party server between your form and your org.

Every middleware tool in this category routes your form responses through a server it controls. This one does not. Responses travel between two parties only: Google, where the form lives, and your Salesforce org. No TwinStack servers or other middleware sit in between.

✓

Direct connection

The Apps Script in your form calls a REST endpoint in your org. Google to Salesforce, with nothing in between.

✓

OAuth 2.0 Client Credentials

Authentication runs against a Salesforce External Client App. No password is ever stored in the script.

✓

Scoped Run-As user

Requests execute as one dedicated user with a dedicated permission set, not as a broad admin.

✓

Field-level security enforced

Every record is created with an insert-as-user operation, so your FLS rules apply to the write.

If Salesforce is unavailable when someone submits, the response is still saved in Google Forms, but it isn't sent again automatically. Check the dashboard's last-sync time to spot any gaps.

The connection's Consumer Key and Secret are stored in the form's script properties. Anyone with edit access to the form can see them, so keep edit access limited to people you trust.

10 FAQ

The questions admins ask first.

Is there a free Google Forms to Salesforce integration?

Yes. Google Form Auto Sync is a free, native Salesforce app on AppExchange. It sends every Google Form response into Salesforce in real time, with no licence fee, no per-response charges and no middleware.

What is the purpose of Google Form Auto Sync?

To eliminate the manual work of getting Google Form responses into Salesforce. It gives teams already using Google Forms a way to capture every submission in their CRM automatically, in real time, without exports, imports, or a paid integration platform.

How does Google Form Auto Sync work?

A small Google Apps Script, included in the package and set up through a guided wizard, runs inside your Google Form. When someone submits, the script securely sends the response to a Salesforce REST endpoint, which creates a record the moment it arrives, typically within seconds.

Can Google Form responses create Leads or Contacts in Salesforce?

Each response is first saved as a Form Response record, so nothing is lost. You then use a record-triggered Flow or assignment rules to create or update Leads, Contacts, Cases or Campaign Members.

Does Google Form Auto Sync work with Nonprofit Cloud and NPSP?

Yes. It works in any Salesforce org, including Nonprofit Cloud and NPSP. Use Flow to route responses to Contacts, Program Enrollments (Nonprofit Cloud), Program Engagements (NPSP with the Program Management Module), Cases or Campaign Members.

How much does Google Form Auto Sync cost?

Nothing. It is a free managed package with no licence fee, no per-response charge, and no tiered pricing.

Is Google Form Auto Sync secure?

Responses travel between two parties only: Google, where the form lives, and your Salesforce org. No TwinStack servers or other middleware sit in between. Authentication uses the OAuth 2.0 Client Credentials Flow against a Salesforce External Client App, requests run under a single dedicated Run-As user, and every record insert enforces field-level security through Salesforce's insert-as-user model.

What happens if Salesforce is unavailable when someone submits a Google Form?

If Salesforce is unavailable when someone submits, the response is still saved in Google Forms, but it isn't sent again automatically. Check the dashboard's last-sync time to spot any gaps.

Do I need a developer to set up Google Form Auto Sync?

No. The in-app 5-step wizard is built for Salesforce admins and includes copy-ready code and credential steps. Setup takes about 15 minutes for your first form and about 5 minutes for each one after that. If you route responses with a Flow, allow extra time to build it.
